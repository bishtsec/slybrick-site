Found a loose brick in ours?

Last changed 16 November 2026.

What this covers.

Anything Slybrick operates: this website and our mail. Client systems are covered by the client's own disclosure policy, never by this page.

How to report.

Write to bisht@slybrick.com with what you found, how you found it, and what an attacker gets. A proof of concept helps; volume does not. We also read .well-known/security.txt.

The rules on our wall.

  • Test only what this page covers.
  • One request at a time. No scanners, no denial of service.
  • No data that is not yours. Stop the moment personal data appears.
  • Give us a reasonable stretch of time before you publish.

What we promise.

We answer every good-faith report within a few business days. We do not take legal action against research that follows the rules above. You may publish once the issue is fixed, and we will credit you by the name you choose if you want credit.

If we disagree.

Say so. We will explain what we did and why, in plain words.
