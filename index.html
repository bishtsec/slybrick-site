What we do

Testing, research and retests.

Each one is small and does one job well. We say that job in the first sentence, every time.

  • Security testing

    Web, API and mobile testing

    We test your product the way an attacker would, and report what we can show: the requests, the response, and what an attacker gets.

    Talk to us about testing

  • Security research

    Research and disclosure

    We hunt bugs in products we did not build and report them through disclosure programs. What the wild teaches us turns up in every test we run for you.

    Talk to us about research

  • Retest and hardening

    Retests after a fix

    A fix that closes one path often leaves its siblings open. After the fix ships, we check the fix and every neighbouring path, so the class is closed and not the instance.

    Talk to us about a retest

The idea

Every wall has one brick that behaves differently.

  • What we look for

    The one check a product forgot to make.

  • How we look

    The way an attacker would, one request at a time.

  • What you get

    A report you can replay: every request, every response.

One clever brick. Each test we run is small and does one job well.

The name is two plain words: sly, for clever, and brick, for the thing you build with. Sly means clever here, never dishonest.
