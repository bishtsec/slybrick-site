The idea

Every wall has one brick that behaves differently.

  • Slybrick Security

    In security, it is the loose one we find.

  • Slybrick Games

    In a game, it is the one with the secret passage behind it.

  • Slybrick Apps

    In an app, it is the small piece the wall was missing.

One clever brick. Each thing we ship is small and does one job well.

The name is two plain words: sly, for clever, and brick, for the thing you build with. Sly means clever here, never dishonest.
