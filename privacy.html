Privacy.

Last changed 16 November 2026.

This site collects nothing.

No analytics. No cookies. No forms. Nothing is stored on your device and nothing is sent anywhere on our behalf. The page you are reading is static: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and fonts served from one host, and nothing else.

Email.

If you write to bisht@slybrick.com, we receive your address and your message. We use them to reply to you. We keep the thread while the conversation is live, and we delete it on request. There is no mailing list.

Engagements.

Client work is governed by a signed agreement, not by this page. Under that agreement: scope and findings stay confidential, report drafts reach the client first, and any personal data that turns up during testing is shown to the client and never copied into reports or published.

Questions or deletions.

Write to the same address and say what you need.
